## A — Veynar the Lich, paperwork

Ah. The hero. Right on schedule — I had it written down somewhere. Under the...
the pile. The pile with the curses in it. Three hundred years I've kept this spire
standing, and do you know what no one tells you about eternal rule? The forms.
Every soul I reap needs a signature, every skeleton a rota, and the ravens keep...
going on strike. I meant to conquer the valley by spring. It's autumn. Again. So
come, swing your sword. Just — give me a moment. I need to sit down. The throne is
cold, the tea is colder, and honestly, hero... if you win, the paperwork is yours.

## B — The dragon and the rescheduled hero

You came on a Tuesday. Heroes used to come on Fridays, with trumpets, and I had
the whole week to... to polish the gold. Now it's Tuesday, the hoard is a mess,
and my left wing clicks when it rains. Four hundred years I have kept this
mountain, and the knights keep getting younger. The last one asked if I took
card. Card! For a ransom! So. Breathe fire, roar, the whole... the whole show.
Only — could we do it sitting down? The ledge is warm, the view is good, and
between us, lad... if you beat me, you inherit the mountain. And the mice. And
the damp. Good luck with the damp.

## C — The necromancer and the skeleton union

No, no — don't bow. The skeletons do that now. They do everything now, since
the... the meeting. I raised an army to take the coast, and the coast is still
there, because my dead went and formed a union. Rotas. Breaks. A banner. "We
rise, we rest." Can you believe it? I gave them eternity and they want weekends.
So here I am, digging my own graves at midnight, one spade, one... one more
spade, while the dead hold a vote on whether to haunt the north field. Hero, if
you're here to end me — wonderful. Take the spade. The union meets at dawn, and
they will want to talk to you.

## D — The demon auditor

Sign here. And here. Initial the clause about your soul — yes, the small print,
everyone misses the small print, that's rather the point. Nine hundred years of
contracts, and do you know what breaks a demon? Not holy water. Audits. The Pit
wants every soul filed in triplicate, and my quill has... has run dry twice this
week. I was going to tempt a kingdom tonight. Instead I'm here, counting yours,
while the infernal post brings more. So take the deal, mortal. Take it and go.
The fine print says you win. It always did. I only ever wanted someone to read
it to the end.

## E — The dark queen and the mirror

Mirror, mirror — oh, forget it. It talks back now. Three centuries of "fairest
of them all" and last week it asked me how I was holding up. Holding up! I have
a curse to renew, a stepson to... to ruin, politely, and a ball to haunt, and the
crows keep bringing me the wrong poison. Apple, it must be apple, I wrote it
down — where did I write it down? Never mind. Come closer, child. The spindle
is sharp, the tower is tall, and I have done this a thousand times. Only this
once... could you prick your own finger? My hands are shaking, and the mirror
is watching, and I refuse to give it the satisfaction.

## F — The warlock on hold with the void

Hold on. Hold on — the void is calling. Again. Third time tonight, and it never
leaves a message, it just... breathes. I sold my name for power when I was
nineteen, and the power is fine, honestly, it's the admin. Every spell needs a
patron's nod, every nod needs a form, and the form needs a soul I already spent.
My master wants the river dammed by dawn. The river! With what — my bare hands?
Look, hero, I know how this ends. You raise the sword, I raise the... the staff.
Only — could we do it tomorrow? The void is on hold, the kettle's on, and I
haven't heard my own name in years.

## G — The vampire lord at dawn

Dawn. Of course it's dawn. They always schedule the final battle at dawn, as if
I keep... as if I keep hours. Six hundred years of night, and the one meeting I
cannot miss is at sunrise, in a courtyard, in the sun. My coffin has a leak, my
cloak is moth-eaten, and the bats — the bats have stopped returning my calls.
I was going to drain the valley tonight. Instead I am counting minutes of dark
like coins. So strike, hero. Strike true. Only... stand in my shadow when you do.
If I crumble, I would rather it was your sword, not the morning, that did it.

## H — The goblin king and the dungeon HOA

Quiet! Quiet, all of you — the hero is here, and the dungeon is a disgrace. Who
left the spikes out? The spikes go in on Tuesdays, we talked about this, we had
a... a meeting. I built this kingdom from a damp hole and three stolen spoons,
and now it's committees. The trolls want a bigger bridge. The traps want oil.
The treasure chest wants a lock that actually... locks. I was going to eat a
kingdom this year. Instead I'm mopping the throne room. So go on, hero, take the
crown. It's tin. It pinches. And the trolls meet on Thursday, and they will want
to talk to you about the bridge.

## I — The witch and the slow cooker

Stir it. No — stir it the other way. The cauldron used to bubble on its own, and
now I have to... to coax it. Four hundred years of curses, and the newt supply
dried up, the eye of newt, the toe of frog, all of it rationed, and the village
below keeps sending me... baskets. Baskets of thanks! For the pox I lifted in
'62. I was going to blight the harvest tonight. Instead I'm simmering soup for
a widow's cold. So drink up, hero, it's only broth. Only — if you mean to end
me, do it after supper. The pot is warm, the bread is warm, and I am... I am
not ready to be cold again.

## J — The dark lord and the postponed final battle

You again. Third time this month, and the last two times you... you rescheduled.
I had the lightning ready. The lightning! I keep it in a jar now, it goes flat
if I leave it out. Nine hundred years I have waited on this mountain for a
hero worth the name, and you lot keep turning up at teatime, unannounced, with
your little swords and your little... speeches. So. Monologue, menace, the
whole... the whole bit. Only — sit down first. The steps are steep, the wind is
up, and honestly, boy... if you kill me tonight, you'll have to do the
monologue tomorrow. To the next one. And the next. Forever.
